# Rubric and rules, from the event slides

Source: "Claude Impact Lab - Digital Health - Nov 21" slide deck.

## Our track

General Medicine. The gap: everyday care runs on manual follow-up. Who feels it: patients and primary care teams. Build toward: triage, follow-up, care coordination. Done means one routine workflow, end to end.

## Judging: five criteria, each scored 1 to 5 by six judges

| Criterion | What the slides say it means |
|---|---|
| Presentation Effectiveness | The problem, the user and the outcome are obvious. |
| Problem Focus | One real problem, for one real person, clearly named. |
| Solution Alignment | The build answers the problem the team named. |
| Clinical Usability and Safety | A person stays in the loop, and the software says what it is. |
| Fable 5.2 Model Innovation | The build leans on what Fable 5.2 does best. |

One winner per track.

## Safety questions to answer before the demo

1. Is any of this real patient data? Synthetic only.
2. Who reviews it before a patient sees it? Name the clinician and show where they step in.
3. What happens when someone is in crisis? Hand off to a person.
4. Does it say what it is? It says it is software, and it does not diagnose.
5. Would the person you built it for trust it?

## Demo format

Three minutes, then two minutes of judge questions. Say four things: team number, track and the person you built for; the build running live; the Fable 5.2 capability it leans on most; what surprised you. The slides also ask where the model fell short.

Upload the demo to the team folder before 2:30.
